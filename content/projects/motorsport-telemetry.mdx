## Facts

- Built a real-time race-telemetry dashboard streaming live IMU acceleration (Ax, Ay, Az), GPS position, and a 720p MJPEG video feed from the vehicle.
- Estimated vehicle speed by fusing noisy IMU and GPS measurements rather than relying on either sensor alone; inferred driving behaviour through acceleration-variance analysis.
- Designed onboard measurement hardware light enough not to affect vehicle dynamics — second-lightest telemetry system at the event.
- Placed 5th overall.
