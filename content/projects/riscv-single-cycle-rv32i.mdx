## Facts

- Implemented a single-cycle RV32I datapath (PC, instruction memory, register file, ALU, control unit) in SystemVerilog, decoding all 10 R-type ops via real opcode/funct3/funct7 encoding — including disambiguating ADD/SUB and SRL/SRA, which share funct3 and differ only in funct7.
- Designed a 10-operation ALU with correct signed vs. unsigned semantics (SLT vs. SLTU, SRA's sign-extension vs. SRL) and a register file with x0 hardwired to zero, combinational reads, and synchronous writes.
- Verified in Vivado behavioral simulation via a backdoor-preloaded testbench (register values can't be loaded through this instruction subset alone): 10/10 destination registers matched expected values, PASS=10, FAIL=0.
