## Facts

- Built a Python/MATLAB signal-processing pipeline to analyze large sampled-signal (noisy audio) datasets in both time and frequency domains.
- Applied FFT-based spectral analysis to identify dominant frequency components and characterize noise across samples.
- Designed and evaluated FIR and IIR digital filters for noise reduction, comparing frequency responses and filtered-signal quality to select the best configuration.
- Investigated sampling, aliasing, cutoff frequency, filter order, and SNR to evaluate the effect of filtering on signal reconstruction.
