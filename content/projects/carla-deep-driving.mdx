## Facts

- Designed a controlled ablation study in the CARLA simulator to isolate the contribution of spatial vs. temporal architectures in predicting throttle, steer, and brake from camera input, holding data and training constant.
- Implemented and trained three models, each as a 3-estimator bagged ensemble: ResNet18 single-frame CNN baseline (11.2M params), ResNet18 + 2-layer GRU on 5-frame sequences (13.5M params), GRU-only with no CNN backbone (77.5M params).
- Found the CNN baseline achieved the lowest validation error (MSE 0.0171, Steer MAE 0.0097) vs. CNN-GRU (MSE 0.0269) and GRU-only (MSE 0.0311) — spatial convolutional features mattered more than temporal sequence modelling for this task.
