## Facts

- Designed and verified a synthesizable AES-128 encryption core (iterative, FSM-driven datapath) in SystemVerilog, targeting hardware/RTL interview prep for networking-hardware control-path design.
- Verified bit-exact output in behavioral simulation against the official FIPS-197 test vector.
- Synthesized out-of-context for a Xilinx Artix-7, analyzing LUT/register utilization and closing static timing at 100MHz — root-caused a routing-delay bottleneck from a high-fanout FSM register and applied a fanout-replication fix, improving worst negative slack from 1.068ns to 3.704ns.
